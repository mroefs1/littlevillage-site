35th Annual Golf Outing
📅 October 3, 2026 at 1:00 PM
📍 Seaford Golf Club, Seaford, NY
📝 Published September 20, 2026
✎ Metadata bar: date, time, location, published date all in one horizontal strip.
event hero image

Join us for the 35th Annual Golf Outing, our flagship fundraiser supporting programs and services for children with developmental delays and disabilities at Hagedorn Little Village School!

What to expect: 18 holes of golf, a catered lunch on the course, a silent auction, raffles, and a awards ceremony. Foursomes, tee sponsorships, and individual player packages are available.

All proceeds benefit Little Village School — 100% of registration fees and sponsorships go directly to student programming, therapy services, and educational resources.

Registration and sponsorship close September 25th.
