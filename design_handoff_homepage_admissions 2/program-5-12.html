Elementary School, for 5 to 12 years
A full academic school day for children whose IEP calls for a specialized, therapeutically supported setting — through elementary school and the transition beyond.
photo — elementary classroom or therapy room
5 – 12 years
A full school day, built around each child's IEP
Certified teachers deliver New York State-aligned academics in small classes with a high staff-to-student ratio, while on-site therapists deliver related services without pulling children out of the building. Transportation is arranged by the district.
A typical school day
8:45 AM
Bus arrival & homeroom.
9:00 AM
Core academics — reading, math, and writing in small groups.
11:00 AM
Related services block — speech, OT, PT, or counseling per the IEP.
12:15 PM
Lunch & recess.
1:00 PM
Specials — art, music, adaptive PE — and afternoon academics.
3:00 PM
Dismissal & bus departure.
Services included
Speech & Language Occupational Therapy Physical Therapy Counseling & Social Work Adaptive Physical Education Transportation
