The enrollment journey
1
Reach out to us
Fill out the request form or call. We'll listen, answer questions, and point you to the right starting place — Early Intervention (under 3) or your district's CPSE (3+).
2
Evaluation & referral
A free evaluation determines your child's needs and results in an IFSP or IEP. Your county or district can refer your child to Little Village — you can request us by name.
3
Visit & meet the team
Tour the school, meet teachers and therapists, and see the classrooms. This is where most families feel their shoulders drop.
4
Placement & first day
Once placement is approved, we handle the logistics — including transportation — and welcome your child in. And we stay in touch with you every step after.
$0
There is no tuition, ever.
As a publicly funded program, all education, therapy, and transportation come at no cost to qualifying families.
