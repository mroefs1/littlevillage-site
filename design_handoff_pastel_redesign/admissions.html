The enrollment journey

1Reach out to us

Fill out the request form or call. We'll listen, answer questions, and point you to the right starting place — Early Intervention (under 3) or your district's CPSE (3+).

2Evaluation & referral

A free evaluation determines your child's needs and results in an IFSP or IEP. Your county or district can refer your child to Little Village.

3Visit & meet the team

Tour the school, meet teachers and therapists, and see the classrooms.

4Placement & first day

We handle the logistics — including transportation — and stay in touch with you every step after.

$0
There is no tuition, ever.

As a publicly funded program, all education, therapy, and transportation come at no cost to qualifying families.
